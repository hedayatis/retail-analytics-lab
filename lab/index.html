03 / Repeat purchasing

How often do customers return?

Cohorts of first observed purchase

Repeat-purchase curve by cohort age

Read the curve correctly

Each point is the share ordering in that month since acquisition, weighted by cohort size. Customers can skip a month and return later: this is not a survival curve.

Only cohorts with a fully observed acquisition month and comparison month contribute. The eligible population changes with age; December 2011 is excluded.

View eligible populations and percentages

Cohort retention heatmap

Full history · percentages of each cohort ordering again. Scroll horizontally for later months. Dots mean not yet observable; * marks a partial observation month. Zero means observed with no returning customers.

04 / Trust & interpretation

What changes the headline numbers?

Rows affected by each cleaning decision

Rule-level counts, not a cumulative funnel. Returns follow a separate branch.

Customer attribution coverage

Sales without a customer identifier contribute to revenue and market totals, but not to RFM or customer cohorts. These views therefore have different denominators.

Revenue is not profit. No costs, margins or causal campaign effects are available in this dataset.

06 / Method & provenance

Inspect the evidence behind the dashboard

The pipeline uses the UCI Online Retail II dataset and established RFM/cohort methods. This is an engineering portfolio implementation, not a claim to have invented those methods or collected the dataset.

RFM is checked across pandas, standard-library Python, DuckDB SQL and base R; cohorts across pandas, SQL and R. Contributions include the pipeline, validation and interface, with AI-assisted development. The uploaded starting package has not received an independent authorship audit.
