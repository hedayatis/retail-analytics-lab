# Revision 1.1.0: Dashboard-first analytical insights

- Date: 2026-08-31
- Status: release
- Previous revision: 1.0.1

## Purpose and scope

Replace the code-first landing experience with a usable analytical dashboard.
Keep editable implementation details and logs available, but collapsed by default.
Fix the source highlighter that replaced comments/strings with unresolved numeric
control-code placeholders. No statistical/model pipeline changes are included.

## Calculations and interpretation

- Selected sales revenue: sum of published monthly clean-sales revenue.
- Selected orders: sum of monthly distinct invoices. The data's invoices are
  monthly transactions; monthly customer counts are deliberately not summed into
  a claim of distinct customers.
- Average order value: selected revenue divided by selected orders, not the
  unweighted mean of monthly averages.
- YoY: latest selected complete month's revenue divided by the same complete
  month in the prior year, minus one. Missing/zero comparator means unavailable.
- Customer and segment revenue shares: separate denominators of all identified
  customers and their attributed sales revenue. They are not shares of all sales.
- Unattributed revenue: total sales revenue minus summed segment revenue.
- Markets: published top-ten revenue plus a residual "Other countries" category,
  all divided by total sales revenue.
- Retention at age k: sum(cohort size × published percentage) / sum(cohort size),
  using only eligible cohorts at that age. Null cells are censored, zero is
  observed zero. Both acquisition and observation months must be fully observed.
  Eligibility therefore changes with age. Input percentages are rounded to two
  decimals, making the weighted curve approximate. It is repeat purchasing in a
  month, not survival; customers may leave and return.
- The heatmap retains the published values and marks partial observation months
  with an asterisk, rather than disguising partial data as complete.
- Cleaning visualization shows separate rule counts. Returns branch from sales,
  so these counts are not presented as one additive funnel or waterfall.
- Reactivation cards report historical spend only, not recoverable revenue or
  estimated causal uplift. Recommendations are hypotheses for future testing.

## Components and compatibility

Browser HTML/CSS/JS, new pure insight/highlighting modules, frontend tests,
CI, README and version metadata. Existing data JSON, R/Python/SQL models, solver
dependencies and public URL are unchanged. Native HTML/SVG and existing browser
runtimes are retained; no chart-framework dependency or hosting migration.
The main portfolio, RIPPLE, ISAPDI and book placement are untouched.

## Migration and limitations

No migration required. First-time R/Python runs download external runtimes and
depend on CDN/network availability. The live sample has 25 customers; it is not a
representative sample for market estimates. Source and data attribution are
explicit, but no independent authorship or novelty audit is claimed. Dataset
source-specific licensing notices are preserved.
